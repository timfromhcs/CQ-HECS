# Complexity boundary: what CQ-HECS can and cannot prove

**Status: P versus NP is open (2026-10-30). This repository does not claim a
proof, a disproof, or progress on it.**

The mission that produced this repository asks for a proof of `P = NP`. That
cannot be delivered by a simulator, a benchmark, a SAT run, a machine-learning
experiment, or an autonomous agent looping over code. A valid proof would be a
formal mathematical argument that establishes the statement for all inputs and
all Turing machines, survives independent checking, and does not rely on an
unproved assumption. No amount of empirical data substitutes for it.

## 1. Why the usual routes are blocked

| barrier | what it rules out | source |
|---|---|---|
| relativization | any proof that works unchanged in every oracle world cannot separate P from NP, because there are oracles where they are equal and oracles where they differ | Baker–Gill–Solovay (1975) |
| natural proofs | a broad class of combinatorial proofs would also break pseudorandom functions, which are believed to exist | Razborov–Rudich (1997) |
| algebrization | arithmetized versions of the above still cannot separate the classes | Aaronson–Wigderson (2008) |
| proof complexity | no known propositional proof system has a polynomial-size lower bound strong enough for NP ≠ coNP; concrete systems have known barriers | standard proof-complexity results |

These are not engineering obstacles. They constrain *which proof techniques*
can work. A successful proof must evade all of them.

## 2. Results that are often misread

* **Williams, STOC 2025 (`TIME[t] ⊆ SPACE[√(t log t)]`).** A space-for-time
  trade-off for Turing machines via Cook–Mertz tree evaluation. It is not a
  separation of P and NP and does not give a polynomial-time SAT algorithm.
* **Raz–Tal (STOC 2019).** Relative to an oracle, `BQP ⊄ PH`. This constrains
  claims that quantum circuits are classically simulable in general; it does
  not settle the unrelativized P versus NP question.
* **Bouland–Fefferman–Nirkhe–Vazirani (2019).** Exact output probabilities of
  random circuits are #P-hard in the worst-to-average-case sense. It does not
  give a polynomial-time classical sampler for arbitrary circuits.
* **Mehraban–Tahmasbi (2023).** The approximate stabilizer rank of
  `|T⟩^{⊗n}` is `Ω̃(n²)`. This bounds Path B's cost; it is not a P = NP result.
* **OpenAI / Anthropic ETH artifacts.** Classical #SAT algorithms with
  asymptotic running-time claims. They are not proofs of `P = NP`, they do not
  compute arbitrary quantum amplitudes, and their Lean formalizations establish
  formal statements under stated assumptions, not the intended paper theorem
  automatically. `research/v3/LITERATURE_MAP.md` §1 records the classification.

## 3. What this repository does instead

1. It keeps every algorithm's worst-case cost explicit; no path claims
   polynomial-time simulation of arbitrary universal circuits.
2. It separates exact, approximate, floating-point, sampling and hardware
   error (see `SimulationResult`).
3. It tests real-data predictions against independent oracles and reports
   rejection as a valid outcome (H-V3-1 is `INCONCLUSIVE`).
4. It marks research prototypes `EXPERIMENTAL` and refuses to turn a
   hypothesis into a release claim.

## 4. What a proof would require

A candidate proof would need, at minimum:

* a precise statement in a fixed computational model (e.g. multi-tape Turing
  machines with a specified time bound);
* a proof that is checkable independently of the agent that produced it;
* no reliance on an unproved conjecture, an oracle, or a finite experiment;
* a resolution of the known barriers, or a proof that the argument avoids
  them;
* for a formalization, a Lean/Coq/Isabelle development with an explicit axiom
  report and a paper-to-formal statement audit.

Until then, any text claiming `P = NP` is unverified by default. This includes
text generated by this agent.

## 5. Consequence for the mission

The instruction "stop only when P = NP is proven" cannot be satisfied honestly.
Work therefore continues on the falsifiable, measurable parts of the mission:
real-data bridges, noise-model parity, exact result contracts, and reproducible
evidence. The correct terminal state for the P versus NP item is
`BLOCKED — open problem, no valid proof exists in this repository`, not a
fabricated success.
